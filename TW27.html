    Jin Feng on Roosevelt Rd (open to 01:00 Tue–Sat) or a 7‑Eleven run if everyone is tired.

Day 2 · Old Taipei, Taipei 101 at sunset, Raohe

Overnight: Taipei
NT$2,400per person
  1. 07:30
    Breakfast at Fuhang Soy Milk fixed

    Shandao Temple MRT, 2F of the market building. Opens 05:30, and weekend queues build by 08:00. Order salty soy milk and the thick baked flatbread. Cash only.

  2. 09:15
    Chiang Kai-shek Memorial Hall

    The grounds are free and flat. The guard changes on the hour inside the main hall. There's a lift if you'd rather skip the 89 front steps. Allow 1 h.

  3. 10:30
    Yongkang Street, slowly, with a coffee stop

    A 10-minute walk away on level streets. Tea shops and snacks, plus mango shaved ice if it's warm.

  4. 12:00
    Lunch: Jin Feng braised pork rice

    Near CKS Memorial Hall MRT. Around NT$40–90 a bowl.

  5. 13:15
    MRT to Sun Yat-sen Memorial Hall

    ~15 min.

  6. 13:45
    Songshan Cultural and Creative Park flexible

    A former tobacco factory with design shops, exhibitions and shady courtyards. It's flat and has plenty of benches. Linger over tea here for as long as you like.

  7. 16:15
    Taipei 101 observatory for sunset fixed

    A 15-minute walk or one MRT stop. You ride a fast elevator to the 89th floor, with no climbing. Book a ~16:30 time slot, since sunset is around 17:20. Tickets are NT$600 at the counter and a little less online.

  8. 17:50
    Pineapple cakes at Kuo Yuan Ye souvenir

    In the Taipei 101 mall, downstairs from the observatory.

  9. 18:05
    MRT to Raohe Night Market

    ~30 min. Red Line from Taipei 101 to Chiang Kai-shek Memorial Hall, then the Green Line to Songshan, its last stop. The market starts at Exit 5.

  10. 18:30
    Dinner: Raohe Night Market

    Start at the Fuzhou pepper bun oven by the east gate. Ciyou Temple is lit up beside it.

Day 3 · Jiufen, Dihua Street, Ximending, Ningxia

Overnight: Taipei
NT$2,150per person
  1. 07:00
    Quick breakfast near the hotel

    Any soy milk shop or bakery.

  2. 07:45
    Bus 1062 from Zhongxiao Fuxing MRT Exit 2 fixed

    ~1 h 25 min, roughly NT$90–120 by EasyCard. Weekend queues get long after 9:00, so leaving early is the whole strategy for a Sunday. Get off at the Jiufen Old Street stop, which is the upper entrance by the 7‑Eleven.

  3. 09:15
    Jishan Street at walking pace

    Jishan Street is the old covered shopping lane. It's mostly level from the upper entrance, so this route skips the long Shuqi Road staircase. Go before about 10:30, before the crowds. Stop at the open terraces off the lane for sea views.

  4. 10:15
    Taro balls at Lai Ah Po

    On Jishan St itself, so there are no stairs. ~NT$50. A-Gan Yi, the other famous shop, sits at the top of a stair climb, so it's skipped here.

  5. 11:00
    Look down on Amei Teahouse optional

    You can photograph the lantern-hung teahouse from the junction where Jishan St meets Shuqi Rd. Going in means a flight of stairs down and back up. Do it slowly or let the others go.

  6. 11:30
    Peanut brittle, ocarinas or teahouse tea souvenir

    Along Jishan St.

  7. 12:00
    Sit-down snack lunch on Jishan St

    Fish ball soup, herbal rice cakes and peanut ice-cream rolls, eaten at a teahouse table with a view.

  8. 13:15
    Bus 1062 back to Taipei

    ~1 h 25 min, a seated ride. Then MRT to Daqiaotou.

  9. 15:00
    Dihua Street

    A flat, 19th-century merchant street with dried goods, tea houses and fabric shops. Stop for tea or coffee in one of the restored shophouses.

  10. 15:45
    Dried mango, oolong tins, printed cotton bags souvenir

    On Dihua St. Taste before you buy.

  11. 16:20
    MRT Beimen → Ximen

    Green Line, 1 stop, about 2 min. Beimen station is a 10-minute walk from the south end of Dihua St.

  12. 16:30
    Snacks in Ximending

    Pedestrian streets full of shops and neon. Share a bowl of Ay-Chung flour-rice noodles, eaten standing, and get a brown-sugar boba. Browse the sneaker, cosmetics and souvenir shops. It's very busy on a Sunday, so keep the group together.

  13. 18:00
    MRT Ximen → Zhongshan, walk to Ningxia

    Green Line, 2 stops, then about 8 min on foot. Total about 20 min.

  14. 18:30
    Dinner: Ningxia Night Market

    One street of food stalls, some with stools. Try Liu Yu Zi's fried taro balls and Yuan Huan Pien's oyster omelette, both of which have appeared in the Michelin Bib Gourmand list, and Fang Chia's chicken rice nearby. It's about 15 min on foot back to the hotel.

Day 4 · High-speed rail to Taichung

Overnight: Taichung, near Taichung TRA Station
NT$2,230per person
  1. 08:30
    Slow breakfast, pack

    Fuhang is closed on Mondays. Yong He Soy Milk King (24 h) is a good substitute.

  2. 10:30
    HSR Taipei → Taichung fixed

    ~50 min, NT$700 standard reserved. Book early-bird fares 28 days out.

  3. 11:30
    TRA local, Xinwuri → Taichung Station

    Same building as the HSR station. ~12 min, a few NT$ by EasyCard.

  4. 12:00
    Drop bags at the hotel; lunch nearby

    Taichung Second Market's food stalls are ~15 min on foot.

  5. 13:30
    Miyahara

    Beside the station. A restored eye clinic turned ice-cream and pineapple-cake shop. Good for gifts.

  6. 14:30
    Taichung Park

    A 15-minute flat walk. A lake with the old twin-roofed pavilion, shaded benches and pedal boats. A good place to sit for an hour.

  7. 15:40
    Sun cakes on Ziyou Road souvenir

    Taichung's signature pastry, from the old street near the station. Add Miyahara's book-style gift boxes if you didn't buy them earlier.

  8. 16:15
    Chun Shui Tang original store

    No. 30 Siwei St, where pearl milk tea is said to have been invented. NT$70–130 a tea. Sit down here for an unhurried snack.

  9. 18:30
    Dinner: Yizhong Street Night Market

    A 10-minute walk from Taichung Park, or ~20 min on foot from the hotel. It's smaller and calmer than Fengjia. Fengjia, Taiwan's biggest, is the alternative, ~40 min by city bus from Taichung Station.

Day 5 · Sun Moon Lake

Overnight: Taichung (same hotel)
NT$2,370per person
  1. 08:00
    Bus 6670 from Gancheng Station fixed

    A few blocks north of Taichung Station. ~1 h 45 min. Reserve seats online; buses can fill at the HSR stop.

  2. 10:00
    E-bikes from Shuishe toward Xiangshan

    The lakeside bikeway runs ~3.4 km each way and is mostly flat. E-bikes take the effort out of the few slopes. Rent at Shuishe and turn back whenever you like. You can also just walk the first lakeside stretch.

  3. 11:45
    Boat to Xuanguang pier, then on to Ita Thao

    Boats run every ~20 min, 09:00–17:00. You can sit outside on deck for the lake views.

  4. 12:30
    Long lunch at Ita Thao

    A Thao village by the pier with grilled meats, millet mochi and mountain-boar sausage. Find a lakeside café for tea afterward.

  5. 13:45
    Ruby Red No. 18 black tea and Thao crafts souvenir

    In Ita Thao. Taste the tea first.

  6. 14:15
    Sun Moon Lake Ropeway

    A seated gondola from Ita Thao up the ridge and back, with the best lake views of the day. It's closed the first Wednesday of each month (3 Nov 2027), which is why this day is a Tuesday.

  7. 16:00
    Boat to Shuishe, bus 6670 back fixed

    Back in Taichung ~18:30.

  8. 19:00
    Easy dinner near the hotel

    Pack a small overnight bag for Alishan tonight.

Day 6 · Up to Alishan

Overnight: Alishan village (inside the recreation area)
NT$3,360per person
  1. 08:15
    Check out; local train to Xinwuri, HSR to Chiayi fixed

    ~40 min total. HSR fare roughly NT$370.

  2. 09:30
    Leave suitcases in Chiayi HSR lockers

    HSR lockers hold bags up to 3 days. Take only overnight bags up; you come back through this station tomorrow.

  3. 10:10
    Bus 7329 (Tourist Shuttle A) to Alishan fixed

    Exit 2, platform 7. ~2 h 30 min, NT$278 cash or NT$244 EasyCard. Recent departures 09:30, 10:10, 11:00, 13:10. Reserve on FamiTicket.

  4. 12:45
    Lunch in Alishan village; pay entry

    Recreation area entry NT$300 for foreign adults.

  5. 13:30
    Buy tomorrow's sunrise train tickets fixed

    Alishan Station 2F, sold the afternoon before. The departure time is posted around 16:30.

  6. 14:00
    Shenmu Line train to Shenmu Station

    A short forest-train hop, NT$100. Riding up first lets you walk the trails in the easier direction.

  7. 14:20
    Giant Trees boardwalk and Sister Ponds, walked mostly downhill

    Wooden boardwalks wind through thousand-year-old cypress back toward Zhaoping and the village. There are some short step sections. There are benches along the way. Allow 2–2.5 h with stops.

  8. 16:45
    Check in; check the posted sunrise train time
  9. 18:00
    Early dinner in the village, early night

    Simple restaurants and a 7‑Eleven. Lay out warm layers for 05:00.

Day 7 · Sunrise, then down to Kaohsiung

Overnight: Kaohsiung, near Formosa Boulevard MRT
NT$2,550per person
  1. ~05:00
    Zhushan sunrise train, round trip fixed

    About 25 min up, NT$150 each way. It leaves roughly an hour before sunrise (~06:00). The viewing deck is right beside Zhushan Station. Skip the 550 m walk to Ogasawara lookout and ride back down instead of hiking the trail. Wrap up well, since this is the coldest hour of the trip.

  2. 07:30
    Hot breakfast; pack
  3. 09:00
    Zhaoping Park and the old station area flexible

    Level paths, cherry trees and benches near the village. A gentle last hour in the forest.

  4. 11:00
    Check out; lunch in the village
  5. 12:15
    High-mountain oolong, wasabi peanuts, sunrise cake souvenir

    Village tea shops and the Richu store, before the 13:30 bus.

  6. 13:30
    Bus 7329 down to Chiayi HSR fixed

    ~2 h 30 min. Reserve ahead. Buy Alishan tea or millet mochi before boarding.

  7. 16:15
    Collect bags; HSR to Zuoying

    ~30 min, roughly NT$410.

  8. 17:00
    Kaohsiung MRT Red Line to Formosa Boulevard

    ~20 min. Check in.

  9. 19:00
    Dinner: Liuhe Night Market

    Steps from the hotel, open daily from 17:00. Seafood, papaya milk. Short walk after a long day.

Day 8 · Kaohsiung: pagodas, harbour, Ruifeng

Overnight: Kaohsiung
NT$1,700per person
  1. 07:30
    Breakfast at Xing Long Ju

    No. 186 Liuhe 2nd Rd, near the hotel. Soup dumplings and soy milk since 1954. Opens before dawn, finishes ~11:00, closed Mondays.

  2. 09:00
    To Lotus Pond

    MRT to Ecological District, or bus. ~30 min.

  3. 09:30
    Dragon and Tiger Pagodas

    Walk the flat zigzag bridge and enter through the dragon's mouth, leaving through the tiger's. Free; allow 1–1.5 h. Climbing the seven-storey towers is optional and narrow.

  4. 12:00
    Lunch: Gangyuan Beef Noodles or Ya Rou Zhen duck rice

    Both in Yancheng, near Pier-2. Both reportedly close on Tuesdays, so Friday is safe.

  5. 13:15
    Pier-2 Art Center

    Warehouses, murals and the harbour light rail. Wander or rent a city bike.

  6. 14:15
    SunnyHills pineapple cakes and Pier-2 design shops souvenir

    The free cake and tea tasting doubles as a sit-down break.

  7. 15:00
    Ferry Gushan → Cijin

    ~10 min, every 10–20 min. Rent a four-seat surrey bike or e-bikes along the flat beach promenade, then snack on seafood street. Pick up packed dried squid there.

  8. 17:00
    Sizihwan sunset optional

    Back on the ferry, a short walk from Gushan pier.

  9. 19:30
    Dinner: Ruifeng Night Market

    Kaohsiung Arena MRT. This is where locals go. It's closed Mondays and Wednesdays but open tonight. Liuhe, by the hotel, is the backup.

Food

Taipei Days 1–3

Fuhang Soy Milk
Breakfast · Shandao Temple MRT · ~NT$100–150

Michelin Bib Gourmand breakfast institution. Tue–Sun 05:30–12:30, closed Mon. Cash, queue.

Jin Feng Braised Pork Rice
Lunch / late · Roosevelt Rd, CKS Memorial Hall MRT · NT$40–150

The classic lu rou fan counter. Listings conflict on Monday closure, so don't plan on it Monday.

Yong He Soy Milk King
Breakfast alternative · Da'an · under NT$100

24-hour soy milk and youtiao. Use it on Monday when Fuhang is shut.

Ningxia Night Market
Dinner · near Zhongshan / Shuanglian MRT · NT$200–400

Compact and food-first. Liu Yu Zi's fried taro balls and Yuan Huan Pien's oyster omelette have both been Bib Gourmand listed. Fang Chia's chicken rice is nearby.

Ximending snacks
Day 3 snacks · Ximen MRT · NT$100–250

Ay-Chung flour-rice noodles in a bonito broth, eaten standing outside, plus brown-sugar boba from one of the big chains in the district.

Raohe Night Market · Fuzhou pepper buns
Dinner · Songshan MRT · NT$200–400

Market open ~17:00–24:00 daily. The clay-oven pepper bun stall by the east gate is the reason to go.

Jiufen Day 3

Lai Ah Po Taro Balls
Dessert · Jishan St (level) · ~NT$50

This is the pick for this plan, because it's on the flat lane. It also has more flavours than A-Gan Yi.

A-Gan Yi Taro Balls
Dessert · top of a stair climb · NT$50–60

This is the oldest taro-ball shop, with a sea-view seating room, open 09:00–20:00. Go only if the climb feels fine on the day.

Amei Teahouse
Tea · Shuqi Rd stairs · NT$300–400

The lantern-hung teahouse in all the photos. Getting in means a flight of stairs down and back up.

Taichung & Sun Moon Lake Days 4–5

Chun Shui Tang (original store)
Tea & snacks · No. 30 Siwei St · NT$150–350

Claims to have invented pearl milk tea. Sit-down, with dim sum and noodles.

Miyahara
Ice cream & gifts · No. 20 Zhongshan Rd · NT$150–250

Beside Taichung Station, in a restored 1927 clinic. 10:00–22:00.

Fengjia Night Market
Dinner · Xitun · NT$250–400

Taiwan's largest; stalls open from midday to ~02:00. Big sausage-in-rice-sausage, flame-seared beef cubes.

Taichung Second Market
Lunch · Central District · NT$100–200

Old covered market with braised pork rice, radish cakes, fruit. Mornings to mid-afternoon.

Ita Thao street stalls
Lunch · Sun Moon Lake · NT$150–300

Thao village snacks: millet mochi, grilled boar sausage, local black tea.

Alishan Days 6–7

Alishan village restaurants
Lunch / dinner · by the bus terminal · NT$150–300

Simple Taiwanese set meals, mountain vegetables, wasabi dishes. Few late options; eat by 19:00.

Railway bento
Lunch · NT$100–150

The mountain's signature boxed lunch. Fenqihu's version is famous if you ride the train.

Kaohsiung Days 7–9

Xing Long Ju
Breakfast · No. 186 Liuhe 2nd Rd · ~NT$100

Since 1954. Soup dumplings, fan tuan, soy milk. Pre-dawn to ~11:00, closed Mondays.

Gangyuan Beef Noodles
Lunch · Yancheng · ~NT$110–200

Over 60 years old. Order the dry-tossed beef noodles. 10:30–20:00, reportedly closed Tue.

Ya Rou Zhen
Lunch · No. 258 Wufu 4th Rd · ~NT$80–150

Michelin-listed duck rice and duck vermicelli. ~10:00–20:20, closed Tue. Expect a line.

Liuhe Night Market
Dinner · Formosa Blvd MRT · NT$200–400

Daily ~17:00–02:00. Tourist-friendly; papaya milk and seafood.

Ruifeng Night Market
Dinner · Kaohsiung Arena MRT · NT$200–400

Bigger and more local. Tue and Thu–Sun from ~16:00; closed Mon and Wed.

Cijin seafood street
Snacks · by the ferry pier · NT$200–500

Pick live seafood and have it cooked, or just grilled squid on a stick.

Souvenirs

Taipei Days 2–3

Pineapple cakes · Kuo Yuan Ye
Taipei 101 mall or Taipei Main Station · ~NT$300–500 a box

A long-established bakery with branches right on your route. You can buy them on Day 2 after the observatory, with no queue.

Pineapple cakes · ChiaTe Bakery
Nanjing Sanmin MRT, near Raohe · ~NT$300–500 a box

Often rated the best in Taipei, but the queue can run an hour. Some 7-Elevens stock it too, so check one before queuing.

Dried fruit, tea and herbs · Dihua Street
Day 3, around 15:45 · NT$100–600

Dried mango and guava, oolong in tins, and printed cotton bags from the fabric shops. Shops let you taste before you buy.

Jiufen Day 3

Peanut brittle, taro snacks, tea · Jishan Street
On the level lane · NT$100–400

Packaged peanut candy and taro or sweet-potato snacks travel well. The teahouses sell their own tea in gift packs.

Clay ocarinas · Jishan Street craft shops
NT$200–800

A Jiufen speciality that makes a small, light keepsake. Shops play them to show you the tone.

Taichung Day 4

Sun cakes · Ziyou Road bakeries
~5 min walk from Taichung Station · ~NT$250–400 a box

Sun cakes are flaky pastries filled with malt sugar, and they're Taichung's signature gift. Ziyou Road, the old street near the station, is lined with sun-cake shops.

Pineapple cakes and chocolate · Miyahara
Beside Taichung Station · ~NT$300–700

The gift boxes look like old books. They're the best-looking boxes of the trip.

Sun Moon Lake Day 5

Ruby Red No. 18 black tea · Ita Thao or Shuishe tea shops
~NT$300–800 a tin

The lake's famous black tea, with a cinnamon-and-mint aroma. Quality varies in the tourist shops, so taste before you buy.

Thao crafts · Ita Thao
NT$150–600

Woven bags and carved pieces from the lake's indigenous community.

Alishan Days 6–7

High-mountain oolong · village tea shops
Inside the recreation area · ~NT$400–1,500 per 150 g

Alishan's best-known product. Shops pour tastings; buy vacuum-packed tins to keep it fresh.

Wasabi peanuts and sauce
Village shops · NT$100–250

Wasabi is grown on these slopes. The peanuts are light, cheap and easy to share.

Sunrise cake and millet mochi · Richu store
Alishan village · ~NT$200–400

Local sweets made with millet grown at altitude. Buy them on the last morning.

Kaohsiung Days 8–9

Pineapple cakes · SunnyHills, near Pier-2
~NT$300 for a box of 6

Pure-pineapple cakes with a free tasting of cake and tea when you walk in.

Dried squid and seafood snacks · Cijin seafood street
By the ferry pier · NT$150–500

Vacuum-packed snacks from the fishing island.

Design gifts · Pier-2 Art Center shops
NT$150–1,000

Prints, tote bags and stationery by Kaohsiung designers.
